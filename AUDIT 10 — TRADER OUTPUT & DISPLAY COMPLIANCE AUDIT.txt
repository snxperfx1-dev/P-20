Tally:
* Diagnostic fields: ~84 / 95 ≈ 88%

Determination: This is overwhelmingly a Wave Diagnostic System, with a thin Market Intelligence layer, and only nascent Trade Decision System characteristics. The "Action" field and "grade" do constitute real decision output, but Entry/Target/Invalidation — the three fields that would make this a genuine Trade Decision System — are absent. The system tells the trader what phase the market is in (88% of the surface area) far more than it tells them what to do about it with specific numbers.
